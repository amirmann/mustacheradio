Privacy Policy

Mustache Radio — Last updated: September 2026

Overview

Mustache Radio is a simple radio streaming app. We take your privacy seriously: we do not collect, store, or share any personal data.

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • No account or sign-in required
  • No personal information (name, email, location, etc.)
  • No usage analytics or tracking
  • No advertising identifiers
  • No crash reporting sent to our servers

Local Storage

The app stores the following data only on your device, never transmitted anywhere:

  • Your preferred app settings (dark mode, "What's Playing" toggle)
  • The last station you played (for ordering the station list)

This data can be cleared at any time by uninstalling the app.

Internet Access

The app connects to the internet solely to stream audio from the radio stations you select. These connections are made directly to the broadcasters' public streaming servers. We have no control over those servers and do not intermediate the connection.

Google Cast

If you use the Cast feature, your device communicates with your Chromecast or Cast-enabled device on your local network. No Cast data passes through our servers.

Third-Party Services

The app uses the following Google libraries:

  • Google Cast SDK — for Chromecast support. Subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Services — required by the Android platform. Subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

Children

This app does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app constitutes acceptance of any updated policy.
